# What Building Things Teaches You

There is a difference between understanding an idea and building something with it.

You can read about a technology, follow tutorials, and understand the terminology. But when you start building, you encounter the decisions and problems that explanations often leave out.

## Learning through problems

Building something forces you to make decisions. You discover what you understand, what you have only memorized, and what you still need to learn.

Errors become part of the process. Sometimes the solution is straightforward. Other times, finding the cause teaches you more than the original task.

## Progress over perfection

Not everything you build needs to become a product. Some projects exist to help you explore an idea, understand a system, or practice a skill.

The important part is to finish enough of the work to learn from it.

## Keep building

I want to keep building projects, exploring software engineering concepts, and writing down the things I learn along the way.

Not every lesson needs to be groundbreaking. Sometimes understanding something a little better is progress enough.
